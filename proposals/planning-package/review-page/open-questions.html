Q9: What delay is a freeze? resolved

The plan promises a dense graph never freezes the page, but its first stop condition was an unsourced 2 seconds, and the viewer's own comment says a 127-box, 246-link graph already takes about 2 s with the fast settings (graph-viewer/src/model.mjs:105). What layout time counts as no freeze?

Blocks: whether the plan stops after measuring or builds force-by-size

Evidence: Decision (technical, agent's): 250 ms of main-thread time per layout, and no re-layout on an update that leaves the nodes and edges unchanged.
