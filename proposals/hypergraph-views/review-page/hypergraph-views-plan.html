Draw facts whole: hypergraph views

One fact often joins several things in named roles. Drawn as pairs of lines it falls apart into fragments; drawn as one box with labelled spokes it stays whole, and facts that share a place or a date connect. This plan adds that drawing to the shared graph viewer and the Representation Router for every project, then uses it in the DoDAF demo.
